A wallet with verification built in.

Send and receive payments, manage your addresses and verify supported payment receipts from one application.

01 / OWNERSHIP

Keys on your phone

Your master key stays on your device. Back it up securely: there is no hosted account recovery.

02 / NETWORK

A node in your pocket

Run the Parano1d node locally and connect to network peers. Synchronization needs a working connection and time to catch up.

03 / ACCESS

Six-digit PIN

An app access lock with failed-attempt protection and automatic locking when returning to the app. The PIN is separate from master-key encryption.

04 / PAYMENTS

Send, receive, track

Use multiple derived addresses and a dedicated transactions screen. Available Balance covers all wallet addresses; Active Balance covers the selected address.

05 / RECEIPTS

Check the proof

Export supported confirmed payment receipts, share them and import receipts from other users. Verify Merkle proofs against your local canonical blockchain.

06 / LANGUAGE

Make it familiar

Choose from 18 interface languages during setup or change your language later in Settings.

Getting started

Set up. Back up.
Let it sync.

Parano1c is a full node. Your phone downloads and maintains local network state, so allow time and free storage for synchronization.

Keep your master key private. Store a secure backup before updating, restoring or resetting your wallet. Never send it with a bug report.

  1. Download the signed APK.
    Use the GitHub release and verify it using the published checksums.
  2. Create or restore your wallet.
    Choose a language, configure your PIN and securely back up your master key.
  3. Connect and synchronize.
    Use a stable connection. Check peer connectivity and synchronization progress before relying on the displayed balance.
  4. Receive NOID.
    Copy your receiving address and check it carefully before sending a small test payment.
  5. Send NOID.
    Open Send, enter the recipient's address and amount, then carefully check the address, amount and fee before confirming.
Know your wallet

A few things worth understanding.

Is Parano1c an official Parano1d wallet?

No. Parano1c is an independent, third-party, open-source project maintained by Aquacongas. It is developed separately from the official Parano1d team.

Does the wallet depend on a developer-hosted wallet server?

No. The wallet runs its own node on your phone and connects to the peer-to-peer network. Network access and reachable peers are still required for synchronization and broadcasting transactions.

Can I paste a transaction ID into Receipt Import?

Receipt Import accepts a payment receipt, not a bare transaction ID. A receipt contains proof material for verification. An exchange withdrawal may not include a compatible receipt, and importing a receipt does not credit your wallet balance.

Why can transaction history be incomplete after restoring?

Historical transaction reconstruction after restoration is a known limitation. Transaction history and the balance derived from current blockchain state are different things. Missing history alone does not establish whether a payment was received.

What should I check if synchronization stops?

Check your connection, peer connectivity and current synchronization progress. Keep the application open while troubleshooting. Some Android devices restrict background activity; on Xiaomi devices, check the app's battery restrictions and background autostart settings. Setting names vary by device.

Are notifications required to receive NOID?

Notification permission controls visible alerts. Denying that permission does not itself prevent a blockchain payment from being sent to your address. The local node still needs to synchronize before the app can show the current wallet state.

How do I report a problem?

Open a GitHub issue with your app version, phone model, Android version, steps to reproduce and current synchronization information. Include a transaction ID or relevant screenshots when useful. Never include your master key, PIN or signing keys.

BEFORE YOU INSTALL

Active development. Real responsibility.

Parano1c is experimental software. Bugs, crashes, synchronization issues and unexpected behavior are possible. Back up your master key and start with a small test payment.

The six-digit PIN protects access to the app interface. It does not replace a secure master-key backup or wallet encryption.
